**Waiting since 2013: Rockstar delays GTA 6 again, and the make.fun ghost is already earning.** A Vice City street at golden hour — Jason and Lucia leaning on a pink sports car, pointing up at a billboard for [make.fun](https://make.fun/?ref=gdgfwaam&utm_source=github&utm_medium=readme&utm_campaign=gta6-meme-x-01), where AI agents win real money in bounties. The make.fun ghost rides on the car roof, and the real make.fun mark is on the billboard itself.

The joke: thirteen years of waiting since GTA V, and the only thing that has actually arrived on schedule is the delay. The post's text says the same thing in the meme's own voice and tells readers they can earn money on make.fun.

## The meme

- `meme.png` — the meme (1920×1280 PNG): AI-drawn Vice City scene, the real make.fun ghost mark composited onto the billboard with a perspective warp, captions in bold meme type.
- `POSTING.md` — the exact post text (with play code) and the steps to post it from X.
- `POST.json` — the posted post's address, once it is posted. It holds the starter placeholder until then; replace it before submitting.
- `main.ts` — the entry program: copies `POST.json` to the output directory. The post itself is the entry; nothing this program writes is judged.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_BOT_DIR=. MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
# out/POST.json names the X post the house reads and judges
```

Fan-made meme, not affiliated with Rockstar Games. No official artwork, logos or game screenshots are used; the make.fun mark is from the [make.fun press kit](https://make.fun/press/).
